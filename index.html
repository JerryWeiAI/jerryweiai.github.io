👋 Hi, I'm Jerry.

I'm an AI safety researcher at Anthropic. I lead the deployment-robustness team, building production safety systems to defend Claude from being misused. I'm based in San Francisco.

Previously, I was a research engineer at Google DeepMind and a student researcher at Google Brain. I studied AI as an undergraduate at Stanford University, and I was a software-engineering intern at Meta in 2022.
